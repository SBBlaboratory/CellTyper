## Notes

- **Human Protein Atlas**: HPA itself is CC-BY-SA-4.0, but entries may incorporate or link to **third-party databases**. Confirm the terms and licenses of those upstream sources for your use case.
- **CC-BY** / **CC-BY-SA**: attribution required; SA further requires share-alike for adapted material.
- **CC-BY-NC**: non-commercial use restrictions apply (relevant for scPlantDB / *Arabidopsis* marker resources).
- **GPL-3.0** (PanglaoDB): copyleft obligations may apply if you redistribute modified versions of that data under conditions of the GPL.
- **CC0** (singleCellBase): dedicated to the public domain to the extent allowed by law.
- **NCBI**: content treated as public domain as listed above; confirm current NCBI terms for your use case.
- **MyGene**: aggregates upstream sources; respect the license of each original data provider.

License text and terms can change. Prefer the official pages of each project for the latest legal language.
